SnappingTool Tab Bridge

Lets the SnappingTool app on this computer save and restore your open tabs.

  1. Open SnappingTool → Settings → Browser extension.
  2. Click Copy pairing code.
  3. Paste it below and click Save & test.

Tab URLs and titles are encrypted with this code and sent only to 127.0.0.1 (this computer). Until a code is saved, nothing is sent.
